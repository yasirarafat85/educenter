# Privacy Policy — Clip Notes

_Last updated: 31 July 2026_

Clip Notes ("the app") is a simple offline notes / clipboard manager that lets you
save text and copy it with one tap. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains
what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Information we collect
None. Clip Notes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We
have no servers and no account system.

## Where your data is stored
Everything you type into the app (your notes, categories, and settings) is stored
only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your phone
through the app. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed.

## Android backup
If you have enabled Google backup on your device, Android may automatically back up
the app's data to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new device. This
is handled entirely by Android/Google under your own Google account — the developer
has no access to it.

## Permissions
The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or
files. It only uses the system clipboard when you tap "Copy".

## Ads and analytics
The app contains no advertisements and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Children
The app is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with
a new "Last updated" date.
